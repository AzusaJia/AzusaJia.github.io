Short Bio

I am an MPhil candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at UNSW Sydney, supervised by Prof. Gustavo Batista. Before coming to UNSW, I completed my BSc at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University. My research interests include reinforcement learning, LLM agents, foundation models for physical systems, and Spatio-temporal Data Mining.

During my first year at UNSW, I explored several directions in time-series classification, including reinforcement learning, spatio-temporal data mining, self-distillation, and few-shot learning. I recently consolidated these work into three manuscripts that are currently under review. I am now working on reinforcement learning for tool-using LLM agents, exploring how informative points along an agent’s trajectory can guide more efficient branch rollouts.

In the longer term, I want to build foundation models that can understand and interact with the physical world. My view is that a world model should represent the physical state of the world, rather than simply its visual appearance. Building on my background in spatio-temporal learning, I am interested in modeling how these states evolve and respond to actions, and in using reinforcement learning to help such models adapt through interaction.

Outside research, I enjoy Gomoku, swimming, and hiking, and I have recently started learning chess. I am also a big fan of Keigo Higashino’s novels—Journey Under the Midnight Sun is my favorite.
