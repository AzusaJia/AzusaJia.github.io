Research

Time series

My earlier research focused on time-series classification, with three manuscripts currently under review. These projects exposed me to reinforcement learning, spatio-temporal data mining, self-distillation, and few-shot learning.

Agentic RL for LLM agents

I currently work on reinforcement learning for tool-using LLM agents. Rather than generating every rollout from scratch, I select informative points along an agent’s trajectory and branch new rollouts from them, making training more efficient.

Physical AI

I am interested in learning systems that can understand and act in the physical world. Building on my background in spatio-temporal learning and reinforcement learning, I hope to develop foundation models for embodied intelligence.
